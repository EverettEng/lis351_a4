Walks by Lake Mendota

This page is about three easy walks near campus.

Get Started

Picnic Point

Picnic Point is a trail that goes out into the lake. It takes about an hour to walk out and back.

Lakeshore Path

The Lakeshore Path is paved. You can start near Memorial Union and walk toward Picnic Point.

James Madison Park

James Madison Park is on the other side of campus. It has a beach and some trees.

Feature: Lakeshore Path

This is the best walk if you only have limited time. The path is flat and does not require a car. From Memorial Union, it takes about 30 minutes to get to Dejope and back.

If you have more time, you can keep walking to Picnic Point, which is an extra 20 minute walk from Dejope.

Before You Go

What to Bring

You should bring:

  • Water
  • Walking shoes
  • A jacket
  • Phone

The Weather

It is colder by the lake than on the rest of campus. In winter the path can be icy, so check before you go.
